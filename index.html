Donation acceptance criteria

  1. Expiry dates. All non-perishable items must have a minimum four-week shelf life remaining before expiration.
  2. Packaging. All goods must be unopened, factory-sealed and undamaged. Minor cosmetic damage to outer packaging is acceptable provided it does not compromise product quality or safety.
  3. Temperature control. Chilled items (below 4°C) and frozen items (below −18°C) must maintain unbroken cold-chain compliance until handover.

This is a test environment. All donor organisations, contact details and delivery notes are fictional and were created by the project team. No real donor, charity or personal data is used.
